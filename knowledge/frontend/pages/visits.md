# Visits

`VisitsPage.tsx` uses `visitsApi` (`/kra/visits`) for create/update/delete and follow-up completion. A new visit requires every field except Add Document. The contact phone is verified with a 6-digit WhatsApp code that expires in 5 minutes, can be resent after 60 seconds, and locks after 5 wrong attempts. The city box shows the full Google Geocoding address, and stays locked when that address is returned. The visits list shows the area and city, with the full address on hover, a map pin when coordinates exist, and a check on that same line when the phone is verified. The location column is wide enough for an area and city with that check. Uploaded documents are shown in View. On a narrow screen each visit is a card, and View scrolls inside the popup. Coordinates and the accuracy radius in meters stay on the line under the box. A fresh GPS watch keeps the tightest fix until the radius is 20 meters or 15 seconds pass. The visit photo uses the latest of those fixes immediately while that watch continues. A radius wider than 50 meters asks the user to retry outdoors. If GPS works but the address cannot be read, the box stays editable and the visit still stores `location_source: gps` with the coordinates. If GPS is unavailable the location can be typed and is stored as `location_source: manual`. The visit photo opens in a large camera overlay, attaches the live preview after the video element is on screen, falls back to any camera when a rear camera is unavailable, and must include GPS coordinates. Preferred brand is a multi-select dropdown for TATA, JSW, AM/NS India, SAIL, NMDC, Apollo, and Others, and Others opens a text box. Customer type is OEM, Fabricator, EPC, Manufacturer, Trader, or Other. Other opens a text box, and that typed type is stored as `customer_type_other` and shown on the visit. Payment terms are a fixed list. The Customer Type and Payment Terms prompts stay visible when the field is empty and are not selectable options. On a new visit, the next purchase date and the follow-up date must be today or later. On a new visit, the captured photo and each added document can be previewed before the visit is saved. The assistant and WhatsApp store a GPS fix or a typed city before they accept the visit photo, and that photo is a camera image that already has coordinates. Updates of existing visits do not repeat this check. Its data mirrors bot visit-agent writes into `customer_visits` and potentially follow-up/KRA data.

A new visit, on the dashboard and on both WhatsApp log paths, requires company, person met, designation, a 10-digit phone, a valid email, city, customer type, visit date, visit type, outcome, probability, product details, monthly consumption, preferred brand, payment terms, delivery location, next purchase date, current requirement, follow-up action, follow-up date, meeting remarks, and one photo or document. The create dialog shows those fields in five steps: company and contact, visit details, commercial intelligence, follow-up, and proof and verify. Next stays on the current step until that step’s existing required fields are filled. Save on the last step still runs the full check and the same create request. Editing an existing visit is unchanged.
