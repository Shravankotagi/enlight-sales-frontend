# Visits

`VisitsPage.tsx` uses `visitsApi` (`/kra/visits`) for create/update/delete and follow-up completion. A new visit requires every field except Add Document. The contact phone is verified with a 6-digit WhatsApp code that expires in 5 minutes, can be resent after 60 seconds, and locks after 5 wrong attempts. The city box shows the city name and stays locked when that name comes from GPS; coordinates stay on the line under the box. If GPS works but the city cannot be read, the box stays editable and the visit still stores `location_source: gps` with the coordinates. If GPS is unavailable the city can be typed and is stored as `location_source: manual`. The visit photo opens in a large camera overlay and must include GPS coordinates. Preferred brand is a multi-select dropdown for TATA, JSW, AM/NS India, SAIL, NMDC, Apollo, and Others, and Others opens a text box. Payment terms are a fixed list. Updates of existing visits do not repeat this check. Its data mirrors bot visit-agent writes into `customer_visits` and potentially follow-up/KRA data.

A new visit, on the dashboard and on both WhatsApp log paths, requires company, person met, designation, a 10-digit phone, a valid email, city, customer type, visit date, visit type, outcome, probability, product details, monthly consumption, preferred brand, payment terms, delivery location, next purchase date, current requirement, follow-up action, follow-up date, meeting remarks, and one photo or document. Editing an existing visit is unchanged.

Preserve date and follow-up field formats. Exercise personal/manager filtering and create/edit/complete transitions when changing this page.
