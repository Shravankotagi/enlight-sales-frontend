# Assistant and intelligence

`AssistantPage.tsx` sends text, image, and GPS payloads with `chatbotApi` to `POST /chat/message`, which the backend forwards to the bot. Choosing Log Field Visit starts the same device location watch as the visit form and shows the reading under the composer. The paperclip offers Upload from device for a PDF only, and Capture an image, which opens the visit camera over the whole page, stamps the GPS, and rejects a photo without coordinates. An uploaded image is refused. Procurement sees AI Assistant in the nav. The suggested prompts and the bot reply use the same interactive buttons as WhatsApp: pending prices, current inquiries, and log a base price. A procurement manager also gets My team. Log a base price lists the first 7 inquiries whose pricing status is exactly awaiting price, as text with the date and time, and says to open the Inquiries tab when more are waiting. A clicked menu button is shown by its name, such as Log a Base Price. Before a base price is saved, the confirmation shows each priced line's amount and the subtotal. An unavailable line has no amount, and the subtotal is ₹0 when nothing is priced. Cancel ends the current step and hi shows the menu again. A question such as how many inquiries a salesperson has left to price is answered with that count. Sales still uses the catalog menu, then *Price this inquiry* after the base price is ready. Update Inquiry is the same catalog as WhatsApp: a row is listed when the sender or the customer owner matches, an `INQ-` code opens it, and a quantity change follows the Price Ready weight rules without moving the deal to Price Quote. Update Order lists the same won deals as the Orders page: a deal the salesperson closed, or a customer assigned to them by the exact company name. A shorter lookalike name does not pull in someone else's order. `IntelligencePage.tsx` presents business intelligence. Accounts Requiring Attention, the reorder queue, and the lost-deals log each show 15 rows per page. Intelligence shows Sync Bigin only for a signed-in admin or salesperson. The backend client uses a 120-second assistant timeout. Interactive chat components may call bot-focused paths depending on deployment configuration.

Keep retries idempotent for mutation-capable messages, avoid logging prompt/image contents, and render model output with the existing markdown safety components rather than raw HTML.
