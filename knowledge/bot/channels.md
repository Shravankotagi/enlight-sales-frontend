# Bot channels and HTTP boundaries

- `index.js`: Express setup, body limits, route mounting, health, import and Bigin sync routes, scheduler start.
- `src/webhook.js`: WhatsApp webhook verification/inbound event handling and message lifecycle.
- `src/whatsapp.js`: WhatsApp Cloud API outbound formatting/sending.
- `src/api/webChat.js`: browser/web chat HTTP interface.
- `src/assemblyai.js`: voice/audio transcription integration.

Keep webhook acknowledgement latency low. WhatsApp processing acknowledges first and continues asynchronously through a per-phone queue, so mutation handling must tolerate redelivery/overlap. Validate webhook handshake/authentication, normalize sender phone numbers once, retain media MIME/type metadata, and make outbound failure handling idempotent. Images can be large: do not log base64 or raw buffers.

## Procurement and sales on the same bot
WhatsApp (`src/webhook.js`) and the dashboard assistant (`src/api/webChat.js`) share one engine. The employee row decides the conversation. `procurement_manager` contains the word `manager`, so the procurement check runs before any manager test, and the bot receives `procurement_person` or `procurement_manager`.
- A procurement person sees inquiries stamped with their employee id, plus unassigned inquiries for the salespeople routed to them. A manager also sees inquiries stamped to the procurement persons who report to them, and the open unassigned pool. The bot treats `new_inquiry` as open as well, because a WhatsApp log uses that status.
- Replies use the procurement number, salesperson, product, quantity, unit, delivery location, and pricing status. They leave out customer name, phone, email, and the raw inquiry text.
- The procurement menu is the same kind of interactive list the salesperson gets. Pending prices are inquiries whose `pricing_status` is exactly `awaiting_price`, matching the Inquiries tab. A blank status stays on the deal stage and is not treated as awaiting a price. All inquiries counts every badge the Inquiries table shows, including zeros: Awaiting Price, Price Ready, and Unavailable when `pricing_status` is one of those, otherwise the deal-stage badge. A historical `awaiting_price` from before `2026-10-15T00:00:00+05:30` uses the deal-stage badge, and `sales_priced` does too. The reply then lists the newest 10 in-scope inquiries, each as its own block: the procurement number on a bold line, then the badge, date and salesperson, product summary, and delivery location. `next` shows the next 10, and the last page says there are no more. Pasting a procurement number or the whole line from that list opens the details and does not ask for a base price. The row id stays `proc_2`. The old phrase `current inquiries` opens the same list. A manager also gets My team. WhatsApp sends that list, and the dashboard assistant renders the same rows as buttons. A clicked row is stored as `proc_1` through `proc_4`, and the assistant shows Pending prices, All inquiries, Log a Base Price, or My team.
- Cancel, stop, or discard ends the current procurement step and shows the menu, the same way the salesperson catalog does. Saying hi after that shows the menu again.
- Log a base price shows the first 7 waiting inquiries as stacked text, newest first. Each block puts the procurement number on its own bold line, then the date and time in India with the salesperson, the delivery location, and the status. Each product is its own bold name, then the specification, the quantity, and the line status (`₹2300/MT`, `Unavailable: not in stock`, or `Awaiting price`). When the quantity is already that weight in MT, the weight is shown once. It does not collapse extra lines or repeat the inquiries as buttons. When more than 7 are waiting, the reply says to view the rest in the Inquiries tab. The list includes an inquiry whose salesperson is known from the stored phone, the customer owner, or the sender, and it skips an awaiting price from before `2026-10-15T00:00:00+05:30`. Sending the number repeats those lines and asks for the price as text: `51000` for one line, or `1 - 2300` and `1 2300, 2 2500, 4 unavailable: not in stock` when there are more. The typed prices, including a comma list, show the same stacked lines and ask `Save this base price?` with Save and Cancel. Save writes each named line and leaves the others unchanged. Cancel writes nothing, and a new price message replaces the preview. It does not offer line buttons, and the line-number reply does not show Save and Cancel. A one-line inquiry still accepts a bare price. The save writes only the named lines and keeps their existing transport, loading, credit, margin, and rate. One priced line makes the inquiry `price_ready`. Every line unavailable makes it `unavailable`. Otherwise it stays `awaiting_price`. After the save the lines are shown again and the inquiry stays open until cancel or the menu.
- Asking for the details or information of a procurement number returns that inquiry: date and time, salesperson, delivery location, pricing status, and every line with its saved base price or unavailable note. It does not open the base-price step. Sending the number alone opens base pricing when that inquiry is awaiting a price. A price-ready or unavailable number does not open or save a base price. While All inquiries is open, the same paste returns the details.
- A procurement user can ask how many inquiries a salesperson in their scope still has left to price, and the same question works for price-ready and unavailable counts. The answer is the salesperson's name and the count. A salesperson outside that scope is refused. A blank or `unavailable` line can take a short note. One priced line makes the inquiry `price_ready`. Every line blank makes it `unavailable`. That save does not send credit, transport, loading, or margin, so it cannot wipe a sales price already stored. If `credit_per_mt` is not on the table yet, the save is retried without that column.
- Procurement cannot log an inquiry, send a quotation, send a decline email, create an order, or open visits, customers, complaints, or KRA. Photos are not used for base prices.
- A salesperson log no longer asks for a rate. A typed price is kept in the notes as a customer target and is not stored as `rate_per_mt`. The inquiry stays `awaiting_price`, keeps its procurement number, and keeps the procurement person copied from the salesperson, so procurement still sees it. After it is saved, the buttons are Log Another Inquiry, Price This Inquiry, and Menu. Price This Inquiry asks for base, transport, loading, credit, and margin on that inquiry. A missing value is asked for again. An explicit blank credit is 0. The entered base is stored as the sales base and does not replace a procurement base already saved.
- Add Price to Inquiry is menu number 5. The later salesperson choices are 6 through 10, and a manager's later choices are 6 through 11. Typing 5 opens that price list. The row ids stay `menu_1` through `menu_10` and `menu_price`. It lists inquiries whose `pricing_status` is exactly `awaiting_price` or `price_ready`, as text with the date, customer, product, and status. Pasting the procurement number, or just its digits, starts the same five-field step. A sentence that names base, transport, loading, credit, and margin is read by those names, including “Base Price”. When every line is complete, Save and Cancel are shown. The dashboard Save button sends `yes`, and `yes` or Save writes the price. The inquiry becomes `sales_priced` and its stage becomes `quoted` (Price Quote) only after that. Negotiation, On Hold, Won, and Lost keep their stage. Cancel leaves the step without writing a price, and hi shows the menu. The existing *Price this inquiry* path still asks only for transport, loading, credit, and margin after procurement has set the base. A blank credit on that path is 0. Quoted, Won, and Lost stay closed until the sales price is saved. An all-unavailable inquiry tells the salesperson to send the decline from the dashboard. The bot does not send that email. An order still needs the saved sales rate. A historical inquiry with no `pricing_status` can still be ordered.

## Bot sales scoping
- `getAccessibleSalespersonPhonesForBot` returns an empty array for procurement roles, so they do not fall through into the sales catalog.
- New inquiries saved by the bot start with `pricing_status = awaiting_price`, a unique `procurement_number`, and the procurement person assigned to that salesperson when one is set.

Bigin import/sync aliases and web-chat API-key/JWT checks are sensitive route boundaries. Do not add privileged unauthenticated routes or assume all aliases have the same protection.
